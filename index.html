How keywords work
Type a keyword and press Enter (or comma). Each one becomes a tag you can remove with its x. Capitals don't matter, and a keyword also matches inside longer words ("ser" finds "server"). No keywords = any message with a Roblox link.
vip the message must contain at least one of the plain keywords
+vip must contain this one (all + keywords are needed)
-scam must NOT contain this one
/regex/ a pattern; works with + and - too

Timed mode

One channel that arms itself again on a timer. While Timed mode is on, the big ARMED button is switched off. It is always off when the app starts.
Arm again after
After it joins one link it stays off for this long, then arms itself again, over and over, until you switch Timed mode off. From 1 second to 24 hours.
